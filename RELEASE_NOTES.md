## Overview

A small tidy-up after an app-wide check of every screen. The check looked for controls that do nothing or repeat
another, and for anything you can see but have to change somewhere else. The few it found are fixed here. Most
screens already let you change things where they're shown.

Install it over the current app as usual. Everything you've logged is kept, and there's no database upgrade.

## Improved

- **Change the workout time from the day log.** Tap the workout's time, sets and volume line under the exercises to
  change its start and finish. A workout timer that's still running is stopped from the ⋮ menu, as before.
- **Edit an exercise's comment from the day log.** Tap the comment under an exercise's sets to edit it, without
  opening the exercise's menu first.
- **A simpler Body tracker menu.** The ⋮ menu only repeated the pencil, so it's gone. The pencil still switches the
  tracker to editing measurements.

## Known limitations

- The check covered controls and editing. Larger changes it suggests, if any, are planned as separate updates.
