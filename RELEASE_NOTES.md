## Overview

Medications now keep track of what you have left, where each injection went and how you feel. FlexNotes counts your
supply down as you take doses and warns you before it runs out or expires, suggests the next injection site on a
body map, and lets you rate symptoms from 0 to 10 beside your doses.

## What's new

- **Supply and refills.** Open a medication and scroll to **Supply** on its Track tab. Choose **Track supply**, enter
  what you have on hand, and FlexNotes subtracts every dose you take from then on. It shows what's left and the day
  it runs out at your current schedule, allowing for off weeks and pauses. **Update supply** adds a refill, recounts, or
  sets an expiry date and how many days' warning you want.
- **Running low.** Any medication that runs low, has run out or expires soon is listed under **Supply** on the
  Medications Track tab, so you see it with today's doses.
- **Injection sites.** For a medication you inject, open its ⋮ menu and choose **Record injection sites**. Taking a
  dose then shows a body map with the abdomen's four quarters, upper arms, thighs and buttocks, with the site used
  longest ago already chosen. Tap another site if you used one. The medication's Track tab shows the map with how
  long ago each site was used, the next one to use and your latest sites.
- **Symptoms.** **Symptoms** on the Medications Track tab opens **Rate symptoms**: add your own (or tap a suggestion
  such as Nausea or Fatigue), then rate each one from 0 to 10 on any day. Ratings save as you tap.
- **Symptoms beside your doses.** Each medication's History shows that day's ratings under the date, and its Graph
  can overlay one symptom on a 0 to 10 scale at the right.

## Improved

- **Change it where it's shown.** Every dose's site appears in the medication's History and can be changed by
  opening the dose. Symptoms can be renamed or deleted from their menu, with Undo, and every supply change has Undo.

## Known limitations

- Supply is counted in the dose unit (for example mg or tablets), so enter what's on hand in that unit.
- A dose taken from the reminder notification records no site. Open it in the medication's History to add one.
- Symptom ratings aren't in the report for a doctor or the CSV yet.
- FlexNotes doesn't give drug interaction or dosing advice.
