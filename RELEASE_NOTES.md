## Overview

Medication reminders are here. When a dose in a running schedule is due, FlexNotes sends a notification you can
answer straight away with **Taken**, **Snooze** or **Skip**, without opening the app. You can also change a dose's
amount for one day or for the rest of a schedule, without editing the schedule itself.

## What's new

- **Dose reminders.** A notification arrives when a scheduled dose is due. Doses due at the same time share one
  notification. **Taken** logs them, **Snooze 15 min** reminds you again shortly, and **Skip** marks them skipped.
  Tap the notification to open Medications.
- **A second reminder.** If a dose still hasn't been taken or skipped, FlexNotes reminds you once more, 30 minutes
  later by default. Change the delay, or turn it off, in Settings.
- **Settings › Medications.** Turn reminders on or off, allow reminders on the exact minute (Android 12 and later
  ask for this), set the second reminder, and choose whether medication names show on the lock screen.
- **Change a dose.** Tap a dose in TODAY and choose **Change amount**. Pick **This day only** or **This day and the
  rest of this schedule**. The schedule keeps its original amounts, and a dose already taken that day takes the new
  amount. Changes are listed in the medication's History and on the schedule's run, and each can be removed, with
  Undo.

## Improved

- **Private by default.** The lock screen says only that a medication reminder is waiting, unless you choose to show
  names.
- **Reliable after a restart.** Reminders are set again after the phone restarts, the time or time zone changes, or
  FlexNotes updates. A reminder disappears by itself once its doses are ticked in the app.
- **Local only.** Reminders are set on your phone. Nothing about your medications leaves it.

## Known limitations

- Without permission for exact alarms, a reminder can arrive up to ten minutes after the dose time.
- A Medications PDF and CSV for your doctor come in the next build.
