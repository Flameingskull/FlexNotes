## Overview

This update rounds out Medications. Your report for a doctor now shows how your symptoms changed and where each
injection went, a dose taken from its reminder records its injection site, and a new emergency card puts your
current medications, allergies and conditions on one page you can save or share.

## What's new

- **Emergency card.** On the Medications screen, open the ⋮ menu and choose **Emergency card**. Enter the name for
  the card, your allergies, conditions and an emergency contact. FlexNotes adds your current medications: those in a
  running schedule, with each dose's amount and time, and anything you logged by hand in the last 30 days. Choose
  **Save PDF** or **Share PDF** for a one-page card, or **Save image** or **Share image** to keep it in your gallery.
  Light by default for printing, or dark.
- **Symptoms in the report for a doctor.** The report has a new **Symptoms** section. For each symptom you rated in
  the chosen dates, it shows how many ratings there are, the average, lowest, highest and latest rating, a small
  graph and any notes. Turn it off like the other sections.
- **Injection sites in the report.** The dose log in the PDF has a **Site** column showing where each injected dose
  went. The CSV file gets a **Site** column too, added at the end so spreadsheets built on earlier exports still work.

## Improved

- **Sites from the reminder.** When you tap **Taken** on a dose reminder for a medication you inject, FlexNotes
  records the site used longest ago, and the button shows which one (for example "Taken · Left thigh"). If two doses
  of it are due at once, they go to different sites. You can change a site afterwards by opening the dose in the
  medication's History.
- **Your details are kept.** What you type on the emergency card is remembered for next time, saved in your
  backups, and kept when you reset settings to their defaults.

## Known limitations

- The emergency card shows only what you entered and logged in FlexNotes. It isn't a medical record, and FlexNotes
  doesn't give drug interaction or dosing advice.
- To choose a different site when taking a dose from its reminder, tap the reminder to open Medications and take the
  dose there, or change the site afterwards in History.
- Medications can't be locked with a PIN or fingerprint yet.
