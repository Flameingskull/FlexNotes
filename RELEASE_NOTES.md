## Overview

This update helps FlexNotes keep you on track outside the app. You can now set **reminders** to weigh in, take
measurements, take progress photos and train, each at your own time and on your own days. A new **home-screen
widget** shows today's doses with a Taken button, and today's workout, without opening the app. As always,
everything runs on your phone, and FlexNotes still has no internet permission.

## What's new

- **Reminders for your logging.** Settings › **Reminders** has four reminders: **Weigh-in**, **Measurements**,
  **Progress photos** and **Workout days**. Switch on the ones you want, then choose a time and the days of the week
  (every day, weekdays, or any days you pick).
- **Tap a reminder to log it.** A weigh-in reminder opens your weight, a measurements reminder opens the Body
  tracker, a photo reminder opens the camera and a workout reminder opens today's log.
- **The workout reminder names your day,** for example "Today: Push Day (PPL)", from the workout you're following.
- **Home-screen widget.** Long-press your home screen, choose Widgets and add **FlexNotes today**. It lists the doses
  still due today, each with **Taken**, your as-needed medications with **Take** (the same amount as last time), and
  today's workout with its training block. Tap the workout or the heading to open FlexNotes.

## Improved

- A reminder stays quiet when that day already has a weight, a progress photo or a logged workout, so you're not
  reminded about something you've done.
- The widget updates as soon as anything changes in the app, and taking a dose from it updates Medications, your
  reminders and your injection sites just as the app does.
- While **Lock Medications** is on, the widget names no medication and hides as-needed medications.

## Known limitations

- The widget shows up to four doses or medications at a time; the rest are counted, and tapping the count opens
  Medications.
- An as-needed medication you haven't taken before, or one at its daily limit, opens Medications from the widget so
  you can choose the amount or confirm.
- Reminders only follow the days and times you set; they don't adapt to a training plan's rest days on their own.
- Medication reminders are unchanged and keep their own page in Settings › Medications.
