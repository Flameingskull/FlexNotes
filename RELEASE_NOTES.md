# FlexNotes 1.0.187

## Overview

This update gives you more say in how FlexNotes behaves. You choose what the calendar shows while Medications is locked, first-run setup asks a few optional facts about you, progress photos can carry a comment, and turning the phone opens a graph full screen.

## What's new

- **Choose how the calendar shows doses while Medications is locked:** hide them (the default, as before), show only how many were taken ("2 doses taken", with no names or amounts), or show them in full. Change it in Settings › Medications. Tapping the count asks to unlock first, then opens Medications.
- **Photo comments:** add a comment to any progress photo from the photo viewer, such as "after the holiday" or "morning, fasted". Edit or clear it any time. It shows under the photo in Compare, goes into exported photos' descriptions and is kept in backups.
- **Turn the phone to open a graph:** with auto-rotate on, turning the phone sideways on the exercise Graph tab, the Body tracker, Medications or Readiness opens the graph full screen. Turning back closes it. A graph you opened with its button stays open. You can switch this off in Settings › Open graphs when you turn the phone.

## Improved

- **Setup asks about you:** first-run setup has a new, optional "You" step for sex, date of birth, height and daily activity, which body fat and calorie estimates use. Every value can be skipped and filled in later under Settings › Profile, and nothing asks for them again.
- **Body measurements have their own setup step,** instead of sitting under Exercises.
- **More room for landscape graphs:** in landscape, a full-screen graph's range and options sit on the top bar, leaving the graph the full height.
- **The pinch-to-zoom hint** on full-screen graphs shows only the first three times.

## Fixed

- **Weekday dates everywhere:** a custom range on Analysis › Breakdown and the date stamps on slideshow and compare videos now read as weekday dates ("Mon 27 Oct"), like the rest of the app.

## Known limitations

- If turning the phone opened a graph, tapping Rotate inside it to go back to portrait also closes it, as turning back would.
- The calendar's count of doses opens Medications, not the day itself in its History.
- The platform update (Kotlin, Android Gradle Plugin, Compose and Room) is planned for the next build.
