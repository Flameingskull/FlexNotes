# FlexNotes 1.0.172

## Overview

You can now log a training session like any other entry: a BJJ class, a tennis match, or how hard a whole gym day felt. FlexNotes then uses how hard each session felt and how long it lasted to track your training load, so you can build up steadily and see when a week jumps too far.

## What's new

- **Session exercises.** Choose the new Session type for an exercise. Each set is one session: how long it ran (in minutes), how hard the whole session felt (0 to 10, with plain words from Rest to Maximal), hard or live minutes, the intensity (Technical, Class, Hard live or Fight pace), the kind of session (Technical, Drilling, Live sparring, Conditioning, Mixed, Competition or Strength), a comment and the done tick. Log as many as you like in a day, and edit, move or delete them on any date.
- **Length from the workout timer.** If you timed the day's workout, a new session starts with that length. You can change it.
- **Cardio zones.** Record the zone you trained in, from 1 to 5. Tap the info button to see what each zone feels like, the talk test, and your own beats-per-minute ranges. Nothing pops up unless you ask.
- **Max heart rate in your profile.** Add it under Settings › Profile if you know it. If you don't, zones use an estimate from your date of birth and say so. Clear it at any time to go back to the estimate.
- **Session load.** Analysis › Readiness can now measure load as Session load: how hard each session felt times its minutes. It shows how this week compares with your usual in plain words (low, a good build-up, take care, or a high jump), with the good range shaded on the graph.
- **Effort against work done.** One graph sets your weekly Session load against your hard minutes and your lifting volume. Tap a week to see its real figures.
- **Jump warning.** The readiness card on the day log tells you when a day's load is more than 50% above your last training day.

## Improved

- **Sessions in workouts.** Add a Session to a workout day with its length, hard minutes, intensity, kind of session and zone already filled in. Rate it when you log it.
- **Graphs and export.** A Session exercise has Session load, Session RPE, Session duration and Hard minutes graphs. CSV export adds hard minutes, intensity, session type, cardio zone and session load columns.
- **Calories.** A session's zone helps estimate its calories when no intensity is set.
- **A second look at unusual sessions.** Saving a session shorter than 15 minutes or longer than 240 minutes asks "Is that right?" once, so a typo doesn't skew your load. A session with no rating shows a quiet reminder to add one, and the load screen says how many sessions aren't counted.

## Your data

- This update upgrades the database. All your sets, workouts and settings are kept, and older backups still restore.

## Known limitations

- A session's rating can't be filled in ahead of time in a workout. You give it when you log the session.
- Intensity and session type are recorded but don't change calories yet. Sports with their own calorie figures come in a later update.
- A Session's Records and Stats still use general time wording, such as "longest".
- The accessibility pass on a real phone (TalkBack, 200% font, small screens and switch access) is still to be done.
