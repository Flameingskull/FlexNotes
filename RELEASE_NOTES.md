# FlexNotes 1.0.178

## Overview

Sparring, bag work and rolls can now be logged the way you train them: in rounds. A new list in the exercise library adds more sports when you want them. The rest timer is also easier to see: its countdown now shows on the lock screen and in the status bar while FlexNotes is minimised.

## What's new

- **Rounds exercises.** A new exercise type for anything done in timed rounds. Log how many rounds you did, how long each one was, the rest between them, how many were hard, how hard it felt overall (RPE) and your cardio zone. FlexNotes works out the total time and hard minutes, and they count toward training load and calories just as a session does. A set reads like "6 × 3 min · 1 min rest · 2 hard · RPE 8". You can also graph rounds, hard rounds and total time. Rounds can be set up in advance in a workout day too.
- **Add sport exercises.** In the exercise library, open the ⋮ menu and choose Add sport exercises to pick from more sports. These include fencing, rugby, volleyball, badminton, CrossFit and Hyrox, plus Rounds starters such as Sparring Rounds, Bag Work, Pad Work and BJJ Rolls. Each one comes with its kind of sport and its Activity set for calories. Nothing is added until you confirm, sports already in your library are marked and left alone, and anything you add can be edited or deleted.

## Improved

- **Rest countdown on the lock screen and in the status bar.** The rest timer's notification now shows in the notification shade and on the lock screen while FlexNotes is minimised, with Pause, +15 s and Stop. It is still silent. On Android 16 the countdown can also appear as a chip in the status bar. If your phone's settings ask, turn on Live notifications for FlexNotes.
- **A clear offer to turn notifications on.** If notifications are off, your first rest timer explains what they're for and offers to turn them on. This also happens when the timer starts on its own after a set. You're only asked once. If you said no before, the button takes you to your phone's notification settings.

## Your data

- No database change. Your sets, exercises, workouts and settings are kept, and older backups still restore.
- After the update, your phone's notification settings for FlexNotes show the Timers channel as new. Any changes you made to the old Timers channel need to be made again.

## Known limitations

- A sport's own counts, such as takedowns, submissions or points, aren't recorded yet (issue #266).
- The status-bar chip depends on Android 16 and the phone maker's support for Live Updates.
- Next up: a review of every screen for clarity and a code clean-up (issues #263 and #264).
