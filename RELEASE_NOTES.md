## Overview

You can now give your doctor a clear record of your medications. The Medications screen creates a PDF with only your
medications, their schedules, how consistently you took them and every dose you logged, and the same dose log as a
spreadsheet. This completes FlexNotes's medications features.

## What's new

- **Report for a doctor.** On the Medications screen, open the ⋮ menu and choose **Report for a doctor**. Pick the
  dates (the last 90 days to start with), which medications, and optionally a patient name, then **Save PDF** or
  **Share PDF**.
- **What the PDF shows.** Your current medications with their latest dose, each schedule with its phases, doses and
  any dose changes, an **adherence** table (doses due, taken, skipped and missed, and the share taken), a graph for
  each medication, a full **dose log** with due and taken times, and your comments. Choose which sections to
  include.
- **Spreadsheet too.** **Save CSV** or **Share CSV** gives the same dose log for a spreadsheet, one row per dose.
- **Quick links.** The Medications ⋮ menu also opens **Settings › Medications**.

## Improved

- **Only what's needed.** The report and spreadsheet hold medication data only, never workouts, body values or
  photos. Light pages print well; dark pages match the app.

## Known limitations

- Adherence counts scheduled doses only; doses you logged by hand appear in the dose log but not in the percentage.
- FlexNotes doesn't give drug interaction or dosing advice.
