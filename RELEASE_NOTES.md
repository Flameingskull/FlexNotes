## Overview

This update makes Medications easier to use, whatever your routine. The screen is now split into tabs, so today's
doses are no longer buried in one long list. A schedule can run with no end date, for medication you take every day
for good. A running schedule can now be corrected: change the day it started, start it again from day 1, or fix and
delete earlier runs.

## What's new

- **Medications in tabs.** The Medications screen now has five tabs: **Today**, **Schedules**, **Medications**,
  **History** and **Graph**. Today shows the doses due, where each schedule is in its cycle, supply warnings and
  symptoms. Schedules lists your schedules with **New schedule**. Medications is the list of your medications, with
  Reorder. The screen opens on Today when a dose is due (including when you tap a reminder), otherwise on Medications.
- **Ongoing doses.** In a schedule, the last phase can now be **Ongoing (no end)**. Its doses carry on every day it
  covers until you end the schedule. Today shows it as "On · day 40 · ongoing", and reminders, supply forecasts, the
  emergency card and the report for a doctor all keep working, however long it runs.
- **Change a schedule's start date.** On a running schedule, tap the **Started** line under RUN (or use its menu) and
  pick a new date. The phases are counted from the new day. Doses you've already ticked stay in your history. Undo
  puts it back.
- **Start again from day 1.** From the same menu, **Start again from day 1** picks a date. The current run ends the day
  before, so its doses and adherence stay in your history. After a schedule has ended, its page offers **Start
  again** with a short explanation.
- **Earlier runs.** A schedule's page lists each run that has ended, with its dates, how many days it ran and the
  share of doses taken. Change a run's first or last day, or delete it, each with Undo.

## Improved

- **Clear empty states.** With no schedule running, Today says so and has a button that goes to Schedules.
- **Safer dates.** Two runs of the same schedule can't overlap, so no dose is ever counted twice.

## Known limitations

- Only the last phase of a schedule can be ongoing, and a schedule that ends in an ongoing phase doesn't repeat.
- Medications taken only when needed, and doses that taper down or step up, can't be scheduled yet. Log them with **+**
  for now. Both are planned.
- A simpler first-schedule setup ("How do you take it?") is planned for a later update.
